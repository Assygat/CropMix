      What the map is — and is not

      • Real administrative boundaries for 2017, simplified. Names shown are current English names; the original 2017 labels are kept in the data.
      • The region outline is drawn from the outer edges of the district layer, so districts and outline match exactly.
      • No fields, farms, crop areas or water use are shown by district: the project has no such data, and the model treats Kyzylorda Region as one unit.
      • Edit map shows a schematic crop mosaic: 1 cell = 100 ha of the region's sown area. Cell positions are illustrative, not actual field locations; only the number of cells per crop changes the scenario.
      • The Syr Darya line is a generalised 1:10m centreline, not the river channel or the canal network.
      • Projection: equirectangular centred on the region; distances are approximate.
