How does CropMix Twin turn river water into a sowing plan?

Weather data becomes the water each crop needs. Losses in fields and canals turn that need into water taken from the Syr Darya. When the river runs short, an optimiser picks the crop mix that keeps the most farmland working. Pick a stage or just watch.
