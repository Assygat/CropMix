Our research

A model built in layers,
with every input documented.

  1. 01

    Crop water demand

    Reference evapotranspiration from the FAO-56 Penman–Monteith equation, multiplied by crop coefficients for eight crop groups grown in the region.

  2. 02

    From field to river

    Effective rainfall, field application efficiency and canal conveyance losses turn crop demand into the water that must leave the river.

  3. 03

    Water-year scenarios

    Low, average and high water years, classified from Syr Darya basin runoff, frame what is realistic in a given season.

Every number is labelled. Inputs are marked real when they come from a cited source and assumed when they do not; results are always modelled.
